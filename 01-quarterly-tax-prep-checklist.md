# QUARTERLY TAX PREP CHECKLIST

## How to use

Work one quarter at a time. Every quarter has the same four moves: **GATHER → REVIEW → SET ASIDE → FILE / PAY**. Check items off as you go. Anything unfinished carries into next quarter's list — it never gets dropped.

Quarters follow the calendar year: Q1 = Jan–Mar, Q2 = Apr–Jun, Q3 = Jul–Sep, Q4 = Oct–Dec. If your jurisdiction uses a different tax year, shift the labels — the rhythm stays the same.

> **Disclaimer:** This checklist is general information only — not tax or legal advice. Deadlines, forms, and thresholds differ by jurisdiction. Confirm every date and requirement with your local tax authority or a qualified accountant.

---

## Q1 (January – March): Close last year, open clean

**GATHER**
- [ ] All income records for last year: invoices sent, payment processor statements, bank deposits
- [ ] All expense receipts for last year, matched line-by-line to your tracker
- [ ] Contractor payment summaries and contracts (whatever your jurisdiction issues)
- [ ] Copy of the prior-year return — file it with this year's records

**REVIEW**
- [ ] Reconcile tracker totals against bank statements: every dollar in and out accounted for
- [ ] Flag missing receipts and request copies now, while vendors still have them
- [ ] Review the deductions log: is every claimed expense documented?

**SET ASIDE**
- [ ] Compute last year's net profit from the Dashboard sheet
- [ ] Set aside the estimated tax owed (use the Tax Estimator sheet) in a separate account

**FILE / PAY**
- [ ] File last year's return by the deadline (or file an extension per your jurisdiction's rules)
- [ ] Pay any balance due; schedule the Q1 estimated payment if your jurisdiction requires quarterly payments

---

## Q2 (April – June): Mid-course correction

**GATHER**
- [ ] Q1 income and expense entries complete in the tracker — no gaps
- [ ] Any new business registrations, licenses, or contractor agreements

**REVIEW**
- [ ] Compare actual Q1 profit against your estimate; adjust the full-year projection
- [ ] Scan expense categories: is any personal spending leaking into business rows?
- [ ] Check the deductible flag on every expense row

**SET ASIDE**
- [ ] Recalculate the quarterly set-aside from the updated profit figure
- [ ] Move the set-aside to your tax savings account the week income lands — not at quarter-end

**FILE / PAY**
- [ ] Make the Q2 estimated payment by your jurisdiction's due date
- [ ] File any jurisdiction-specific mid-year forms

---

## Q3 (July – September): The discipline quarter

**GATHER**
- [ ] Q2 entries complete; receipts scanned and filed
- [ ] Asset purchases this year (equipment, annual software plans) logged with receipts

**REVIEW**
- [ ] Nine-month profit vs. plan — on track or drifting?
- [ ] Audit your own records: pick 10 random expenses — can you produce the receipt in under 2 minutes?
- [ ] Update the deductions log; remove anything you cannot document

**SET ASIDE**
- [ ] Adjust the set-aside rate if income grew or shrank

**FILE / PAY**
- [ ] Make the Q3 estimated payment by the due date
- [ ] Note next year's license/registration renewals and their costs

---

## Q4 (October – December): Finish strong, plan ahead

**GATHER**
- [ ] Q3 entries complete
- [ ] Year-to-date totals from the Dashboard sheet
- [ ] Decide on planned year-end purchases (equipment, prepaying tools) before December

**REVIEW**
- [ ] Full-year profit projection and final tax estimate
- [ ] Review the entire deductions log once more for documentation gaps
- [ ] Decide: does next year need an accountant, or does this system still cover you?

**SET ASIDE**
- [ ] Top up the tax savings account to cover the full estimated liability
- [ ] Set next year's quarterly set-aside rate from this year's actuals

**FILE / PAY**
- [ ] Make the Q4 estimated payment by the due date
- [ ] Calendar next year's filing deadline NOW — with a reminder 2 weeks early

---

## Year-end file box (keep together, every year)

- Final return copy + proof of filing
- Full-year tracker export (save the XLSX — don't rely on memory)
- Deductions log with receipts
- Bank and payment-processor annual statements
- Contracts for the year's major clients
